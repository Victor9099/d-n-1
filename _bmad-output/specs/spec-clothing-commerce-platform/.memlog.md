- (direction) Create an express spec from the final Architecture Spine; adopt the spine as a required companion and preserve its AD invariants without re-deciding them.
- (capability) CAP-1 intent: Shoppers can discover published clothing products and editorial posts through a crawlable storefront. Success: unpublished or archived content never appears publicly, published URLs resolve, and slug changes preserve redirects.
- (capability) CAP-2 intent: Staff can manage products, size-color SKUs, VND prices, stock, lifecycle state and merchandising media. Success: authorized changes round-trip through the contract and public availability is always calculated per SKU.
- (capability) CAP-3 intent: Staff can create, preview, publish, archive and redirect editorial posts. Success: protected draft previews work, publication invalidates affected storefront content, and archived posts remain absent publicly.
- (capability) CAP-4 intent: Shoppers can maintain a cart and obtain a server-authoritative expiring checkout quote. Success: the quote snapshots item subtotal, configured shipping, discounts, tax and grand total in integer VND and is repriced before placement.
- (capability) CAP-5 intent: Guests or customers can place idempotent COD or VNPAY orders without overselling. Success: concurrent last-SKU checkouts yield at most available stock, valid payment confirms one fulfillable settlement, and duplicate or late captures become refundable rather than duplicate fulfillment.
- (capability) CAP-6 intent: Guests can securely track orders and verified customer accounts can claim eligible orders. Success: lookup resists enumeration, claim replay is idempotent, ownership cannot be silently reassigned, and historical order snapshots never change.
- (capability) CAP-7 intent: Authorized staff can process orders through payment, fulfillment, delivery failure, cancellation and refund-required workflows. Success: every accepted transition satisfies the canonical guard/effect table and every rejected transition returns a stable error without changing state.
- (capability) CAP-8 intent: The system authenticates customers and staff and enforces deny-by-default permissions on every protected operation. Success: OwnerAdmin, CatalogEditor and OrderOperator pass only their allowed API scenarios; UI visibility never grants authority.
- (capability) CAP-9 intent: Staff can upload and attach product/post media without routing binary payloads through the commerce API. Success: only finalized validated assets can be attached, public delivery uses object storage/CDN URLs, and orphan cleanup is safely retryable.
- (capability) CAP-10 intent: Business changes can trigger reliable asynchronous expiry, email, media cleanup and cache revalidation effects. Success: crash/retry tests produce one reconciled external effect per event or an auditable dead-letter outcome.
- (capability) CAP-11 intent: Frontend, backend, database and worker agents can build concurrently against stable owned contracts. Success: canonical domain, HTTP, authorization and event contracts pass baseline review, generate matching clients/mocks, and block incompatible or breaking changes.
- (capability) CAP-12 intent: Contributors can run the complete platform locally and produce reviewable atomic changes. Success: Docker Compose reaches healthy local services and commit/CI gates reject invalid commit format, contract drift, lint, type or affected-test failures.
- (constraint) Architecture Spine AD-1 through AD-17 are binding and adopted, including modular-monolith boundaries, cross-module ownership, transaction/lock rules, state invariants, money representation and local callback modes.
- (constraint) MVP market is Vietnam; external canonical money is integer VND while the VNPAY adapter alone translates the provider x100 wire amount.
- (constraint) Product price and stock belong to size-color Variant/SKU; Product is merchandising information and cannot be used as a purchasable stock unit.
- (constraint) Public Product/Post lifecycle is draft to published to archived; draft preview is protected, slug changes retain redirects, and order-referenced records are not hard-deleted.
- (constraint) Guest checkout and optional customer accounts are required; order and line snapshots are immutable history.
- (constraint) Local Docker Compose is the only current runtime target; production infrastructure and operations are outside this spec.
- (constraint) Implementation agents are gated until domain-model.md, api-contract.md, authorization.md and domain-events.md are baselined; executable OpenAPI and event schemas must be generated and validated before app implementation.
- (decision) Spec-authored companions: domain-model.md, api-contract.md, authorization.md and domain-events.md; final Architecture Spine is an adopted companion and remains the authority for architecture and stack.

- (event) spec finalized
- (decision) Reservation TTL for unpaid online checkout is 30 minutes from creation using database time; expiry releases reserved SKU quantities idempotently.
- (decision) MVP shipping rule is a flat 30000 VND fee, waived when merchandise itemSubtotal is at least 500000 VND; backend alone calculates the fee.
- (decision) MVP customer account authentication uses email OTP without password or phone OTP; OTP is single-use and expires after 15 minutes.
- (decision) Guest order lookup and claim tokens are delivered by email, are single-use, and expire after 15 minutes; stored values are hashes only.
- (event) Spec update coherence: PASS — four open questions resolved; capability IDs and intent/success pairs preserved; no assumptions or open questions remain.
- (event) Spec update preservation: PASS — reservation TTL, shipping threshold, email OTP and email token delivery rules landed in SPEC.md and the owning domain, authorization and API companions.
- (decision) VNPAY browser return is display-only but establishes a short-lived order-scoped Secure/HttpOnly checkout-continuation session, strips provider query fields via redirect, and GET /checkout/payment-status returns a redacted versioned projection; no guessable order code or return payload authorizes status reads.
- (decision) Guest lookup redemption consumes the single-use token atomically, establishes a short-lived order-scoped Secure/HttpOnly guest-view session, removes token material from URL/history, applies Referrer-Policy no-referrer, and supports safe reload/back until session expiry.
- (decision) Add non-enumerating POST /me/orders/claim-challenges and an authenticated claim redemption operation; challenge is sent only to immutable order snapshot email, uses 15-minute single-use hash-stored token, and returns stable mismatch/already-owned/already-claimed/expired/replay outcomes.
- (decision) Add a server-guarded operation to create a new immutable VNPAY PaymentAttempt for an eligible unsettled order; every retry gets a new provider reference and idempotency key and returns stable expired-stock, already-settled, or concurrent-retry errors.
- (decision) Idempotency lifecycle: one key per immutable submission intent, persisted through timeout/reload until resolved; payload mismatch is rejected; rotate only after a material edit, a new quote, or an explicitly new payment/refund/admin command.
- (decision) Quote and InventoryReservation use distinct IDs and expiresAt values; quote never implies held stock, online reservation begins only at order placement, and clients use serverTime/version rather than local clocks to accept expiry or regress state.
- (decision) Payment/order status projections include aggregateVersion, serverTime, order status, payment status, fulfillment status, reservation expiry and permitted next actions; clients accept versions monotonically, abort superseded polls and stop at terminal state.
- (decision) A late valid VNPAY capture after reservation expiry confirms paid/confirmed if stock reacquisition succeeds; if reacquisition fails it becomes refund_required. Both branches are returned and emailed from server-confirmed state.
- (decision) Email OTP and lookup/claim challenges are non-enumerating; resendAfter is server-provided, a new challenge invalidates older codes, verification allows at most five attempts, and creation is limited to five per normalized identifier and IP per 15 minutes.
- (decision) Raw OTP/lookup/claim tokens are prohibited from logs, telemetry, Referer headers, localStorage, sessionStorage and debug or animation attributes; redemption routes load no third-party resources before token removal.
- (assumption) MVP staff authentication uses a separate email-OTP challenge for active allowlisted StaffAccount records, with the same 15-minute single-use and rate-limit rules; successful verification establishes a revocable staff HttpOnly session.
- (decision) MVP shipping configuration is read-only in admin at fixed 30000 VND and free from 500000 VND; future edits require a versioned pricing-contract change.
- (decision) Refund operations expose not_requested, requesting, reconciling and dead_letter action substates while canonical payment stays refund_required until provider verification; duplicate actions reuse the original idempotency result.
- (decision) Protected frontend caches are scoped by authenticated subject and permission projection and cleared on logout, account switch, session rotation, role change, and 401/403 before rendering another subject.
- (decision) Minimum Product authoring fields are name, slug, description, lifecycle, finalized media and size-color SKU rows with SKU code, integer VND price and stock; minimum Post fields are title, slug, excerpt, body, lifecycle and finalized cover/media.
- (decision) PII projections: guest sees redacted tracking-only fields; Customer sees owned order snapshots; OrderOperator and OwnerAdmin see fulfillment-required recipient/contact/address with audit; CatalogEditor sees no order PII.
